Snowfall
Genre: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Created by: John Singleton, Eric Amadio, and Dave Andron

🌆 Overview
“What happens when your dream becomes your nightmare?”

Snowfall is a crime drama television series set in 1980s Los Angeles, at the dawn of the crack cocaine epidemic. The story follows Franklin Saint, a young entrepreneur who becomes entangled in the world of drugs, power, and money. The series explores how the drug trade impacts not just Franklin’s life, but also the government, the CIA, and entire communities.

👤 Main Characters

Franklin Saint — A brilliant young man who builds a drug empire.

Teddy McDonald — A CIA operative involved in funding covert missions.

Gustavo “El Oso” Zapata — A Mexican wrestler turned drug enforcer.

Lucia Villanueva — A cartel member tied to Gustavo.

Cissy Saint — Franklin’s caring but conflicted mother.

⚖️ Key Themes

Power and corruption

Economic inequality

The American Dream gone wrong

The human cost of the drug trade
